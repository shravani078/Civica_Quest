Learn About Indian Elections

What is Model Code of Conduct?

The Model Code of Conduct (MCC) is a set of guidelines issued by the Election Commission of India. It comes into force on announcement of the election schedule.

  • No misuse of official machinery for campaigning
  • No announcement of financial grants
  • No hate speech or communal appeals
  • Level playing field for all parties

Role of Election Commission of India

The Election Commission of India (ECI) is an autonomous constitutional authority responsible for administering Union and State election processes.

  • Superintendence, direction, and control of elections
  • Preparation of electoral rolls
  • Registration of political parties
  • Allotment of election symbols

First-Past-The-Post System

India follows the First-Past-The-Post (FPTP) electoral system, also known as simple majority system.

  • Candidate with highest votes wins
  • No need for 50%+ majority
  • Used in Lok Sabha and Vidhan Sabha elections

Why VVPAT is Used?

VVPAT (Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail) provides feedback to voters using EVMs.

  • Voters can verify their vote
  • Paper slip displayed for 7 seconds
  • Stored in sealed box for audit
  • Increases transparency

Indelible Ink

Indelible ink is applied on the left index finger of voters to prevent multiple voting.

  • Stays on finger for several weeks
  • Manufactured by Mysore Paints & Varnish Ltd
  • Contains Silver Nitrate
  • ECI certified standard
